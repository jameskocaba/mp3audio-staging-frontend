How to Record & Save Tracks

On Desktop (Chrome / Edge):

  1. Tap Record Music Tab (or Open Audio): Open your music stream in another browser tab, then tap Record Music Tab (or Open Audio to load an existing file).
  2. Choose what to record: Pick your music tab from the browser prompt and make sure "Share tab audio" is checked.
  3. Press Record: Tap the red Record button and press Play in your music tab.
  4. Navigate back to the MP3aud.io tab: Return to this tab to watch the Left & Right recording bars and continuous stereo wave live while songs auto-split on quiet gaps.
  5. Save your tracks: Tap Save Track to Device (or choose a Save Folder for automatic saving).

🏃 Marathon Recording (12+ Hours / Multi-Day — Zero Browser Storage):

  1. Click 🏃 Marathon (12h+) (or pick a 📁 Save Folder on your local drive / external USB drive in the top bar).
  2. Track-by-Track Flush: With Auto-Save Live to Folder and Track-by-Track Flush (12h+ Safe) enabled in ✂️ Settings, every finished song is written directly to your folder and immediately evicted from browser RAM (0 bytes of browser storage).
  3. Gapless Mixes & DJ Sets: Adjust Max Segment Split (Gapless Cap) in ✂️ Settings (5–60 min, default 15 min) so continuous streams with no silence gaps still split and flush RAM automatically.
  4. Large Multi-Day Track Lists: Once more than 50 tracks are flushed to disk, the Tracks list automatically shows the Latest 50 to keep the page fast — use Load Earlier 50, Show All, or Show Latest 50 to browse older saved tracks (and click ▶ Play to play them back from your folder).
  5. Firefox / Safari Desktop: Click 🏃 Marathon (12h+) or enable Auto-Download Live Tracks in ✂️ Settings to auto-download each finished song and free RAM without a folder picker.

On Mobile (iPhone / Android):

  • Tap Tap to Record to capture live audio from your phone microphone or external audio interface.
  • Or tap Open Audio to load a song/mix from your phone, view the Left & Right wave, auto-split tracks, and save MP3s.

About MP3aud.io

MP3aud.io is a 100% client-side browser audio recorder with a continuous live stereo recording wave, Left & Right channel bars, and Audacity-style track splitting. Capture, editing, ID3 tagging, and MP3/WAV encoding all run locally on your device with zero uploads.

Privacy Policy

All audio recording, editing, and MP3/WAV conversion stays completely inside your browser memory. Nothing is ever uploaded to any server. Saved files go directly to your chosen folder or device Downloads.
