    Cellular Ringtone Setup

    Set as Cellular Phone Ringtone

    Follow these step-by-step directions to install your converted audio as a custom ringtone, text alert, or alarm tone on your phone.

    💡

    No Computer Needed! Uses Apple's free GarageBand app (available free in the App Store). Apple requires custom ringtones to be under 40 seconds.

    1
    Save Download to Files

    Tap Download on MP3aud.io. When prompted by Safari, tap Download to save the audio into your iPhone's Files app (under Downloads).

    2
    Open GarageBand & Audio Recorder

    Open the free GarageBand app. Swipe through the instruments until you see Audio Recorder, then tap it.

    3
    Switch to Tracks View

    Tap the Tracks icon near the top-left (looks like a wall of horizontal audio bars, 3rd icon from the left) to view the project timeline.

    4
    Import Converted Audio

    Tap the Loop icon (top right, next to gear) → tap the Files tab → tap "Browse items from the Files app" → touch & drag your downloaded audio file onto the timeline track.

    5
    Save Project to "My Songs"

    Tap the downward triangle icon in the top-left corner → tap My Songs. Your project will appear in your GarageBand browser.

    6
    Share as Ringtone

    Long-press (touch and hold) your song project → tap Share → choose Ringtone → enter a name for your ringtone → tap Export in the top right.

    7
    Set as Default Ringtone

    When the export succeeds, tap "Use sound as..." → select Standard Ringtone, Text Tone, or Assign to Contact!

    ⚡

    Fast Cable Sync! Transfer the generated .m4r ringtone directly to your iPhone using Finder (Mac) or iTunes / Apple Devices (Windows).

    1
    Download .M4R File on Computer

    Download the ringtone package from MP3aud.io to your computer and locate the .m4r file.

    2
    Connect iPhone via Cable

    Plug your iPhone into your Mac or PC using your Lightning or USB-C charging cable.

    3
    Drag & Drop to iPhone

    On Mac (Finder): Open Finder → click your iPhone in the left sidebar → drag and drop the .m4r file directly onto the iPhone window.
    On Windows: Open iTunes (or Apple Devices app) → select your device → click Tones → drag the .m4r file into the list.

    4
    Select on iPhone

    On your iPhone, open Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone. Your new custom tone will appear at the very top under Custom!

    🤖

    Universal MP3 Support! Android lets you set any MP3 file as your phone ringtone, notification tone, or alarm sound directly in Settings with no extra software needed.

    1
    Download the MP3 Track

    Tap Download on MP3aud.io to save your converted MP3 file to your phone's Downloads folder.

    2
    Open Sound Settings

    Open your Android phone's Settings app → scroll down and tap Sound & vibration (or Sounds and vibration on Samsung Galaxy).

    3
    Select Phone Ringtone

    Tap Phone ringtone (or choose SIM 1 / SIM 2 if you have dual SIM cards active).

    4
    Add Custom Audio Track

    On Samsung Galaxy: Tap the + (plus icon) in the top-right corner of the screen.
    On Google Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus: Tap My Sounds → tap the + Add Ringtone button at the bottom.

    5
    Pick Track & Save

    Select your downloaded MP3 audio file from your files or music picker and tap Done or Save. Your ringtone is now set!

    VIP Pro-Tip

    Want a unique ringtone for specific people? Open your Contacts app → select a contact → tap Edit (or More) → tap Ringtone and choose your custom sound so you know who is calling before looking at your screen!
