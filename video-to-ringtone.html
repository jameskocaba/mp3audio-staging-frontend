Ringtone Maker & Setup Guide

Learn how to convert any video or audio clip on MP3aud.io and install it as your custom ringtone, text tone, or alarm on Apple iPhone (iOS) and Android smartphones.

The Easiest On-Device iPhone Method! Apple requires all custom ringtones to be under 40 seconds (we recommend 25–30s). Using the free GarageBand app (pre-installed or free from the App Store), you can create and set custom ringtones without ever touching a computer.

1
Convert Video & Save to Files

Go to the MP3aud.io Converter, upload your video, and tap Convert. In Mobile Safari, tap Download to save the audio into your iPhone's Files app (under Downloads).

2
Open GarageBand & Choose Audio Recorder

Launch the free GarageBand app on your iPhone. Swipe through the instruments until you find Audio Recorder, then tap it.

3
Switch to Project Timeline View

Look at the top-left toolbar and tap the Tracks View icon (the 3rd icon from the left, looks like small horizontal audio track bars) to enter multitrack timeline view.

4
Import Audio from Files

Tap the Loop Browser icon (near the top-right, next to the gear icon) → tap the Files tab → tap "Browse items from the Files app" → touch and drag your converted audio file directly onto the timeline.

5
Trim & Save to "My Songs"

Ensure the clip is under 30 seconds. Tap the downward navigation triangle in the top-left corner → select My Songs to save your project.

6
Share Project as Ringtone

Touch and hold (long press) your project in the GarageBand browser → tap Share → choose Ringtone → name your tone → tap Export in the top right corner.

7
Assign as Your Phone Ringtone

Once export finishes, tap "Use sound as..." → choose Standard Ringtone, Text Tone, or Assign to Contact. Your ringtone is now live!

⚡

Fast Cable Drag & Drop: If you're on a laptop or desktop computer, transfer the generated .m4r file directly to your connected iPhone using Finder or iTunes.

1
Convert & Save .M4R File on Computer

Convert your video on MP3aud.io and tap Convert to download the archive containing the native .m4r iPhone ringtone file.

2
Connect iPhone via USB Cable

Connect your iPhone to your Mac or Windows PC using your charging cable.

3
Drag .M4R onto Device

On Mac: Open Finder → click your iPhone in the left sidebar → drag and drop the .m4r file directly onto the window.
On Windows: Open iTunes / Apple Devices → select device → click Tones → drop the .m4r file into the list.

4
Apply on iPhone

On your phone, go to Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone. Your custom tone will be waiting right at the top under Custom.

🤖

Native MP3 Compatibility: Android natively supports custom MP3 files for ringtones, notifications, and alarms without needing any 3rd-party apps or computer syncing.

1
Convert & Save the MP3 Track

Convert your video on MP3aud.io and tap Convert to save the audio file directly into your phone's Downloads folder.

2
Open Sound & Vibration Settings

Open your Android phone's Settings app → tap Sound & vibration (or Sounds and vibration on Samsung Galaxy).

3
Tap Phone Ringtone

Select Phone ringtone (or SIM 1 / SIM 2 if you use a dual-SIM setup).

4
Tap the Plus (+) / Add Ringtone Button

On Samsung Galaxy: Tap the + button in the top-right corner.
On Google Pixel / Motorola / OnePlus: Tap My Sounds → tap + Add Ringtone.

5
Select Your Converted Audio

Select your converted MP3 file from your audio picker and tap Done. Your new ringtone is active immediately!

VIP Pro-Tip

Want a personalized ringtone for a specific contact? Open your Contacts app → tap the contact → tap Edit → tap Ringtone and choose your custom tone!
