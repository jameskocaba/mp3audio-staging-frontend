📱 How to Set Your Track as a Phone Ringtone

Toggle 📱 Ringtone in the top chat bar (or text "Make me a ringtone of...") to get both Apple iPhone (.m4r) and Android (.mp3) ringtone files.

🍏 Apple iPhone (No Computer Needed via GarageBand)

  1. Download your track from the chat bubble to your iPhone Files app.
  2. Open Apple's free GarageBand app → tap Audio Recorder → tap the Tracks icon (top left).
  3. Tap the Loop icon (top right) → Files → drag your downloaded file onto the timeline.
  4. Tap the top-left arrow → My Songs, long-press your song → Share → Ringtone → Export!

🤖 Android Smartphones

  1. Tap Download MP3 on any track bubble in the chat.
  2. Open Settings → Sound & vibration → Phone ringtone.
  3. Tap + Add Ringtone (or My Sounds) and choose your downloaded MP3!
