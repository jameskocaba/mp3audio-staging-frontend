How to Record & Save Tracks

On Desktop (Chrome / Edge):

  1. Open your music stream in another browser tab.
  2. Tap Record Music Tab (or the red Record button), pick that tab, and check "Share tab audio".
  3. Press Play in your music tab — watch the Left & Right recording bars and continuous stereo wave live while songs auto-split on quiet gaps.
  4. Tap Save Track to Hard Drive (or choose a Save Folder for automatic saving).

On Mobile (iPhone / Android):

  • Tap Tap to Record to capture live audio from your phone microphone or external audio interface.
  • Or tap Open Audio to load a song/mix from your phone, view the Left & Right wave, auto-split tracks, and save MP3s.

About MP3aud.io

MP3aud.io is a 100% client-side browser audio recorder with a continuous live stereo recording wave, Left & Right channel bars, and Audacity-style track splitting. Capture, editing, ID3 tagging, and MP3/WAV encoding all run locally on your device with zero uploads.

Privacy Policy

All audio recording, editing, and MP3/WAV conversion stays completely inside your browser memory. Nothing is ever uploaded to any server. Saved files go directly to your chosen folder or device Downloads.
